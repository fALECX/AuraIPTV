Privacy Policy for Aura IPTV

Last updated: March 02, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Aura IPTV (the "App") handles your information. Aura is an open-source client application and does not provide any content or media.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Aura is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our own servers. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact information.
  • We do not track your viewing habits or playlist history on our servers.
  • We do not share any information with third-party advertisers.

2. Local Data Storage

Any data you provide to the App (such as IPTV provider URLs, usernames, and passwords) is stored locally on your device. This data is used only to connect to your IPTV provider and is never shared with us or any other third party.

3. Third-Party Services

When you connect to an IPTV provider using the Xtream Codes API, your information is sent directly to that provider. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of your chosen IPTV provider.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect your information, we cannot notify you directly, so we encourage you to review this page periodically.
