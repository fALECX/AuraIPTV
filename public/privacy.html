Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Aura ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

1. Data Collection

Aura is designed as a local media player. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to our own servers. All credentials (URLs, usernames, passwords) and playback history are stored strictly on your device.

2. Third-Party Services

Aura connects to third-party IPTV providers determined by you. When you connect to a provider, your credentials and request data are sent directly to that provider. We do not have access to this information. Please refer to your IPTV provider's privacy policy for information on how they handle your data.

3. Data Security

Sensitive information like your login passwords are encrypted before being stored in your device's local storage to prevent unauthorized local access.

4. Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any data, we do not knowingly collect information from children. However, the content accessed through the app is determined by the user's provider.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
